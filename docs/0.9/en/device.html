Device settings

Look up the gPlug's address and connection

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug I want to look up the address it is reachable at and how good its Wi-Fi reception is, so that I can find it again and choose a good spot for it.

How to do it

  1. Tap Device at the bottom. The Connection card is open.
  2. It shows the Host (the address ending in .local), the IP, the Wi-Fi with its signal strength and the selected Profile.

How you know it worked

  • The address shown also opens the app on another device in the same Wi-Fi.

Notes

  • The signal strength is given in dBm: −50 is very good, below −80 the connection becomes unreliable.
  • Every card on the Device tab opens and closes with a tap on its title; the browser remembers your choice.

Checked with the device simulator on 30 September 2026.

Change the Wi-Fi

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug I want to move it to another Wi-Fi network, for example after changing my router, without setting it up again.

How to do it

  1. Tap Device and, on the Connection card, Change Wi-Fi.
  2. You see the current connection. Tap Change Wi-Fi again; the gPlug searches for networks in range.
  3. Tap the new network, enter the Password and tap Connect. If the network is hidden, choose Enter network manually.
  4. After Connecting…, Connected appears with network, IP and signal.

How you know it worked

  • Connected shows the new network.
  • With a wrong password, Connection failed. Check the password. appears and the gPlug stays reachable.

Notes

  • If the new network is not the one your phone is on, connect the phone to it afterwards and open the address again.

Check or replace the stored key

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug with an encrypted meter I want to check whether the stored key matches the one in my grid operator's letter, and replace it if needed, without ever seeing it in plain text.

How to do it

  1. On the Device tab the Key (GUEK) card shows whether the key is valid or invalid.
  2. To compare, open #setup/meter (the gPlug's address with #setup/meter appended). Use the stored key is switched on and shows an ID of the stored key.
  3. Open Compare with the grid operator's letter, enter the key from the letter and tap Check.
  4. To replace the key, switch Use the stored key off, enter the new one under Key (GUEK) and tap Next.

How you know it worked

  • The comparison shows Matches the stored key. or Does not match. Turn the switch off and enter the key from the letter..
  • After replacing it, the setup check shows Data received.

Notes

  • Stored on the device only. Never shown in plain text or exported.

Send meter values over MQTT

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug with a home automation system of my own (Node-RED, ioBroker, InfluxDB) I want to send the meter values to my MQTT broker, in the format my system expects.

How to do it

  1. On the Device tab, open the MQTT card and switch Enable MQTT on.
  2. Enter the Broker (name or IP) and Port, if needed User (optional) and Password (optional), and the Interval (seconds).
  3. Choose a Template: JSON, one message, One topic per value or InfluxDB line protocol. If you need more, edit Topic and Message yourself; Placeholders lists the placeholders.
  4. Preview with the current values shows exactly what would be sent.
  5. Optional: Send device status also sends the gPlug's memory, firmware, Wi-Fi and diagnosis on a topic of its own; the Availability topic reports online and offline.
  6. Tap Save.

How you know it worked

  • After Saved the card title shows connected, and messages arrive on the broker.
  • A mistake in a template appears below the field with its position and reason, for example “Topic, character 12: unknown placeholder or register”, and Save stays disabled until it is fixed.
  • If the gPlug cannot reach the broker, the card title shows the reason, such as Broker unreachable or Login refused.
  • If a template no longer fits after a change of meter profile, the title shows paused until you adapt the template.

Notes

  • Home Assistant does not need MQTT; it connects to the gPlug directly.

Update the firmware

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug I want to install new firmware without connecting a cable, so that my device gets bug fixes and new features.

How to do it

  1. On the Device tab, open the Firmware and restart card. It shows the installed version.
  2. Tap Check for updates. If there is a newer version, a line such as Update available: 0.6.0 → 0.7.0 appears with the link What's new.
  3. Tap Install 0.7.0 and confirm with Install now.
  4. The gPlug downloads the firmware itself and restarts. Keep it powered.

How you know it worked

  • The card shows Firmware updated and the new version.
  • Settings and history are kept; only the quarter hour in progress is lost.

Notes

  • The gPlug never checks for updates on its own, only when you tap Check for updates.
  • To install a firmware file from your computer, use Choose firmware file and Install. Take the file in OTA format (.ota.bin).
  • If an OTA password is set, the card asks for the OTA password.

Restart the gPlug

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug I want to restart it from the app without pulling the plug.

How to do it

  1. On the Device tab, open the Firmware and restart card.
  2. Tap Restart device and confirm with Really restart?.

How you know it worked

  • After a few seconds the card reports Device is back; the Uptime starts again from zero.
  • The event log records the restart.

Notes

  • Live data pauses briefly, and the last hour's per-minute values and the 24 h memory trend start over.

View the event log and memory

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug I want to see what happened on the device – such as why it restarted – so that I can report a problem or make sense of it myself.

How to do it

  1. On the Device tab, open the Event log card. It lists the last events with their time, for example Restart, Wi-Fi lost or No meter data.
  2. Copy log or Save as file lets you pass the log on.
  3. The Memory card shows Free, Minimum since start and Largest block, and the trend of the last 24 hours.

How you know it worked

  • The log is kept across restarts and gives the reason for each restart.

Notes

  • A line that keeps falling over days means the firmware is leaking memory. A restart clears the trend.

Change the language and appearance

What it is about

As a user of the app I want to choose the language and a light or dark appearance, so that the app is comfortable to read.

How to do it

  1. On the Device tab, open the Language card and tap Deutsch, Français, Italiano or English.
  2. Open the Appearance card and choose Light or Dark.

How you know it worked

  • The app switches at once, without reloading.
  • The choice applies to this browser; another phone keeps its own.

Open the setup assistant again

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug I want to change the model or meter profile later, for example after the meter has been replaced, without setting everything up again.

How to do it

  1. Append one of the following to the gPlug's address and open it:
    • #setup – the whole assistant from Welcome
    • #setup/hardware – straight to the Device step
    • #setup/meter – straight to the Smart meter step
    • #setup/key – to the Smart meter step, with an empty key field
  2. Change what is needed and continue with Next up to Done.

How you know it worked

  • The setup check shows Data received; the history is kept.

Notes

  • The buttons on the Check the setup card lead to exactly these addresses.

Put the gPlug back into setup mode

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug that can no longer reach its Wi-Fi (new router, changed password), I want to put it back into setup mode so that I can connect it again.

How to do it

  1. Hold the button on the gPlug for at least 3 seconds.
  2. The gPlug forgets its Wi-Fi credentials, restarts and opens the gPlug-Setup network again; the light blinks blue.
  3. Connect it as in the first setup and tap Save.

How you know it worked

  • The gPlug is reachable in the new Wi-Fi.
  • Model, meter profile, key and history are kept; the assistant does not have to be run again.
