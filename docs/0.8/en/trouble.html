When something is wrong

“Waiting for the first data” does not go away

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug I want to know how long the check may take, so that I do not change anything too early.

How to do it

  1. After the meter profile has been saved, Connection to the meter shows Waiting for the first data from the meter… with a seconds counter.
  2. Wait up to a minute. Some meters send only every 10 to 30 seconds.

How you know it worked

  • After a minute at the latest, Data received appears, or the Check the setup card with one of the messages in this chapter.

Checked with the device simulator on 30 September 2026.

“No signals from the meter”

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug that receives nothing at all from the meter, I want to know what to check.

How to do it

  1. The Check the setup card shows No signals from the meter; the top right shows No data.
  2. Check that the gPlug sits firmly in the customer interface and that the cable is undamaged.
  3. Tap Check device and connection and check that the right model is selected.
  4. If still nothing arrives, ask your grid operator whether the customer interface is enabled. Until then you can carry on with Continue to the live view anyway.

How you know it worked

  • After the correction the setup check shows Data received.

Checked with the device simulator on 30 September 2026.

“Data unreadable”

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug that receives data it cannot read, I want to know which setting is wrong.

How to do it

  1. The Check the setup card shows Data unreadable.
  2. Tap Change meter profile and choose a different profile; the gPlug suggests the protocol it recognised.
  3. If that does not help, check the selected model with Check device and connection.

How you know it worked

  • After the correction the setup check shows Data received.

Checked with the device simulator on 30 September 2026.

“Profile does not match the meter”

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug who chose a profile with the wrong protocol, I want to get to the right profile quickly.

How to do it

  1. The Check the setup card shows Profile does not match the meter; the top right shows Wrong profile. This message comes at once, without waiting.
  2. Tap Change meter profile. The Smart meter step suggests the profile that fits the detected meter.
  3. Confirm it, enter the key if needed and tap Next.

How you know it worked

  • After the correction the setup check shows Data received.

Checked with the device simulator on 30 September 2026.

“No matching values”

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug whose meter sends readable data that contains none of the expected values, I want to find the profile that fits my meter.

How to do it

  1. The Check the setup card shows No matching values; the top right shows No values.
  2. Tap Change meter profile and choose your grid operator's profile. Show all profiles shows every profile.

How you know it worked

  • After the correction the setup check shows Data received.

Notes

  • The Data tab shows what the meter actually sends.

Checked with the device simulator on 30 September 2026.

“Key does not fit”

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug with an encrypted meter whose data cannot be decrypted, I want to enter the right key.

How to do it

  1. The Check the setup card shows Key does not fit; the top right shows Key invalid. On the Device tab the Key (GUEK) card shows invalid.
  2. Tap Enter the key again. The key field is empty.
  3. Enter the key (GUEK) from your grid operator's letter – 32 characters, without spaces – and tap Next.

How you know it worked

  • After the correction the setup check shows Data received, and the Key (GUEK) card shows valid.

Notes

  • Do not confuse the GUEK with other keys in the letter (such as the authentication key).

Checked with the device simulator on 30 September 2026.
