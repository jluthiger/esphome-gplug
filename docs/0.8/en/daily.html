Everyday use

Read the current power and the meter readings

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug I want to see at a glance how much electricity I am drawing or feeding in right now, so that I understand my consumption.

How to do it

  1. Open the gPlug's address. The app starts on the Live tab.
  2. At the top is the Active power 1.7.0 / 2.7.0 in kW with its direction: Import from the grid or Export to the grid.
  3. The chart below shows the last hour (−60 min to now). Tap or hover over the chart to read a point in time.
  4. The Import 1.8.0 and Export 2.8.0 cards show the meter readings in kWh, as the meter displays them.
  5. With three-phase profiles, Phases · 21/41/61.7.0 shows power, voltage and current per phase.

How you know it worked

  • Data current is shown at the top right; the values update every few seconds.
  • The meter readings match the meter's display.

Notes

  • After the gPlug restarts, the part of the chart before the restart comes from the device's storage (one average per 15 minutes).

Checked with the device simulator on 30 September 2026. Checked on real hardware: gPlugK 2026-09-14.

View energy per day, week, month and year

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug I want to see how much energy I imported and exported over a day, a week, a month or a year, so that I can compare periods.

How to do it

  1. Tap History at the bottom.
  2. Choose the period at the top: Day, Week, Month or Year.
  3. The Energy per interval chart shows import upwards and export downwards. Below it are the totals Import, Export and Total.
  4. Tap Registers (current) to see all current values of the meter with their OBIS codes.

How you know it worked

  • Every period shows a chart and the three totals.
  • Total is import minus export.

Notes

  • The gPlug stores one value every 15 minutes. It keeps about a year and keeps it across restarts and firmware updates.
  • Timestamps estimated (recorded before the clock was synchronised). appears when values were recorded before the gPlug had the time from the internet.

Checked with the device simulator on 30 September 2026.

Export the load profile as CSV

What it is about

As the owner of a gPlug I want to download the stored 15-minute values as a file, so that I can check my electricity bill or settle within a self-consumption group.

How to do it

  1. Tap History and open the Export load profile card. Even closed, its row shows which period is stored.
  2. Choose From and To.
  3. Tap Download CSV.

How you know it worked

  • The browser saves a CSV file with meter readings, energy and power per 15 minutes, separated by semicolons.
  • If From is after To, “From” must be before “To”. appears and nothing is downloaded.

Notes

  • Export everything, including intervals without a timestamp also includes intervals without a timestamp.

Checked with the device simulator on 30 September 2026.

View, copy and save the received raw data

What it is about

As a technically minded owner of a gPlug I want to see the data the meter actually sends, so that I can show what arrives when there is a problem.

How to do it

  1. Tap Data at the bottom. You see the last frames with their time and CRC OK or CRC FAIL.
  2. Choose the view at the top: for encrypted meters Raw · encrypted or Decrypted, for DSMR meters Hex or Plain text.
  3. The Live capture running switch freezes the view so you can read in peace; the gPlug keeps recording.
  4. Tap a frame to open it; it offers Copy and Frame as .txt.
  5. Tick frames or tap Select all and download them in the Export selection card.

How you know it worked

  • A copied or saved frame contains the bytes as hex text with timestamp and CRC.

Notes

  • The key is never exported.

Checked with the device simulator on 30 September 2026.
