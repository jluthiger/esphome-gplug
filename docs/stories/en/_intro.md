# gPlug user manual

The gPlug reads the customer interface of your smart meter and shows power, meter readings and
history in an app that runs on the device itself – in the browser of your phone or computer, with no
cloud and no account.

This manual is organised by task. Each task starts with what you want to achieve, walks you through
the screens step by step and ends with how you can tell that it worked. Labels such as {{S.next}}
are exactly the texts you see in the app.

New here? Start with **First setup**.
